Material exchange

Surplus from one lab becomes a resource for another. Listings below are seeded from waste streams reported in the needs assessment.

Scope. Open to non-hazardous items: consumables, filament, glassware, electronics, unopened supplies. Opened chemicals transfer within the same institute only, subject to EHS approval. Purity and compliance were the top concerns labs raised, so every listing carries condition and custody notes.

Offer an item

Photo (optional, helps people recognise the item)

Hazardous or opened chemicals are limited automatically to internal transfer within the same institute and need EHS approval before collection.

Request an item

Looking for something? Post it here and any lab with spare stock can fill it. It goes through the same custody steps as an offer.

Requests for opened or hazardous chemicals can only be filled from within your institute and still need EHS approval.

When a lab offers to fill a request, the item is listed and marked Requested on your behalf, then moves through Approved → Collected like any other transfer. The request closes as Fulfilled on collection.

Every transfer moves through Requested → Approved → Collected, timestamped, so the giving and receiving labs and EHS share one custody record. In this demo you play both sides.

Why this platform

Needs assessment of 13 labs across QEERI, GORD, and TAMUQ (October 2026). The design decisions on every other page trace back to these findings.

How labs track waste today

10 of 13 labs run on paper, spreadsheets, or nothing. That is the gap this platform fills, and why friction had to be the first design constraint.

Most requested features

The three most requested features map one-to-one onto the site: the dashboard, the exchange portal, and the expiry watch.

How we calculate Placeholder costs

Course toolHow the platform uses it
Life cycle assessmentBoundary: cradle-to-gate production avoided + disposal avoided; functional unit 1 kg. CO₂e avoided = Σ kg reused × reuse factor (kg CO₂e/kg). Reuse avoids making a new item and landfilling the old one; recycling only avoids part of production, so it carries a smaller factor. Landfill is used as the avoided route because it is the main disposal route in Qatar.
Source: UK Government GHG Conversion Factors for Company Reporting 2026, Department for Energy Security and Net Zero (DESNZ), flat file v1.2 (July 2026): “Material use” and “Waste disposal” factors, per tonne ÷ 1000. Estimate for chemicals, which have no published factor.
Circular economy & waste hierarchyFeatures follow the hierarchy: prevent (check before you buy, expiry watch), reuse (exchange), recycle (sorting guide, recycling bins), dispose last (tracked bins, EHS pickups). The dashboard bar shows where each month’s kg ended up.
System dynamicsAdoption is a reinforcing loop: more listings → more useful searches → more users → more listings. Seeded listings and expiry alerts get the loop turning; the 30-second QR log keeps the balancing loop of staff time weak.

Emission factors are published DESNZ 2026 values (UK averages applied to Qatar, so treat totals as indicative); chemicals use an estimate. Disposal costs (QAR/kg) are still placeholders until EHS confirms its rates.
